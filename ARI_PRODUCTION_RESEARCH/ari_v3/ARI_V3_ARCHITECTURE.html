LLM-Powered Query Agents NEW
Text2CypherGenerator
Neo4j Queries
Converts natural language to Cypher queries for ANY field.

"Gucci bags under $500"
→ WHERE extracted_brand = 'Gucci'
  AND price < 500
Works for: brand, color, price, style, material...
SemanticQueryGenerator
Qdrant Queries
Expands queries with style understanding and synonyms.

"boho chic dress"
→ "bohemian relaxed flowy
  earthy natural free-spirited"
Extracts: styles, colors, occasion, price hints
Visual Fusion Strategies
weighted_average
Combines semantic and visual scores with configurable weights.
score = 0.6 * semantic + 0.4 * visual
max
Takes the higher score from either search.
score = max(semantic, visual)
cascade
Visual re-ranks semantic results.
semantic_top → visual_rerank
